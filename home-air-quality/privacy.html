Privacy policy

Home Air Quality Test uses Google account authorization to read the thermostat data the account owner chooses to share. It also uses an authorized Google Cloud Pub/Sub subscription to receive device state-change events.

Data and purpose

Collected data includes device and zone identifiers, reported HVAC state, thermostat mode, temperature, humidity, available setpoints, fan timer state, connectivity, timestamps and collection health. These observations support personal research into indoor air-quality changes.

Storage and access

Raw events and snapshots are archived in private Google Drive storage. Observations, derived intervals and health records are stored in a private Google Sheet. OAuth credentials are kept in the collector’s restricted Apps Script properties and private local setup files. Access is limited to the project owner and people the owner explicitly authorizes.

Google provides the account authorization, device API, messaging, storage and execution services used by the collector. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. This public website does not receive or display thermostat observations or OAuth credentials. The website’s hosting provider may process routine connection information to serve these pages.

Retention and deletion

Raw archives and derived observations are retained for longitudinal research until the owner deletes them. Recent processing ledgers are periodically trimmed while archives are retained. The account owner can revoke access through Google account permissions, stop collection and delete stored observations, archives and credentials. Revoking authorization stops future access but does not automatically delete earlier observations.

Home Air Quality Test’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This policy will be updated if the project’s data practices change.

Questions or deletion requests: contact the project maintainer (do not include device readings or credentials in public issues).
