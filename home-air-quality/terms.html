Terms of use

Home Air Quality Test is a personal research collector for the account owner’s authorized devices. It is not offered as a public thermostat service.

Only authorize accounts, devices and cloud resources you own or are permitted to access. The collector observes reported device state and does not change thermostat settings.

Observations and derived durations can be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Fan timer state is not measured blower runtime. Research outputs are not intended for safety monitoring, emergency response, medical decisions or equipment control.

The project is provided as available, without a promise of uninterrupted collection or complete historical data. The owner may stop or change collection at any time. Google services remain subject to Google’s applicable terms.

Use of collected Google data follows the privacy policy.

Questions or deletion requests: contact the project maintainer (do not include device readings or credentials in public issues).
