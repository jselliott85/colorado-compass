Home Air Quality Test

A personal research project examining how indoor air quality changes with weather, heating and household activity.

The collector reads authorized Google Nest thermostat observations and combines them with a private air-quality dataset. It records reported heating, cooling and off states, thermostat mode, temperature, humidity, available setpoints, fan timer state and collection health.

The integration only observes the thermostat. It does not change settings or send equipment-control commands. Fan timer state does not measure physical blower operation. Missing observations remain unknown.

This website describes the project. Account authorization happens through Google, and collection runs separately. Thermostat readings and account credentials are not displayed here.

Questions or deletion requests: contact the project maintainer (do not include device readings or credentials in public issues).
